Metric definitions

room_time_percent: Scheduled session minutes / (all saved rooms × available weekly teaching minutes). Breaks in named periods are excluded. Low usage is not proof of poor timetable quality.

occupied_room_seat_percent: Student-group size × session minutes / assigned-room capacity × session minutes; unused room periods excluded.

preference_satisfaction: Each assigned session with approved preferred windows for its instructor is counted once; satisfied if it fits entirely within at least one window. Per-rule counts separately evaluate each window. Unused-instructor rules are not applicable.

conflicts: Counts overlapping assignment pairs independently for shared room, instructor and any student group. A pair can appear in several categories; categories must not be added as a unique-conflict count.

Limitations

  • Draft only; not publication-ready.
  • Weekly workload limits, daily limits and assignment locks are not evaluated.
  • Teaching-minute totals are descriptive, not compliance with undefined workload limits.
  • No overall efficiency/fairness score or educational improvement is claimed.
